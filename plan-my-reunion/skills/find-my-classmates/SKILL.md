# Find my classmates

Use this when an organizer with a Plan My Reunion reunion wants help finding
their classmates or building the class list. Our team also works on every
class list; this lets the organizer go faster.

1. **Start from the list.** Call `list_classmates` (just the missing ones by
   default) so you don't look for people already found, and note the class
   size if there is one.
2. **Use what the organizer has first.** A yearbook page, a graduation
   program, a list from a friend: read the names and add them with
   `add_classmates`. One yearbook beats hours of searching.
3. **Then public pages,** for the school and class year:
   - the local paper's commencement coverage and honors (valedictorian,
     scholarships, National Merit);
   - public sports and activity rosters from their senior year;
   - the class's public Facebook group or page.
   Show the organizer what you found and ask before adding it.
4. **Finding a particular classmate:** a public Facebook, Instagram or
   LinkedIn profile counts only with two signals that it's them (the school,
   the hometown, classmates in common). Save the profile link, nothing from
   inside it.
5. **Add in batches** of up to 100 with `add_classmates`: first and last name
   (and the last name at graduation when it changed), the city if known, the
   profile link if found, and `foundOn`, the page where you found them. The
   list only fills gaps; nobody is overwritten or duplicated.
6. **Wrap up** with the counts from the tool and the Classmates page link.
   Suggest sharing the class page: classmates add themselves and tell us
   where others are.

Rules:

- Never save or guess emails, phone numbers or home addresses. Classmates add
  their own contact details when they RSVP; the organizer can add ones they
  already have on the Classmates page.
- Public pages only: never log in anywhere, get past a paywall, or use
  people-search or data-broker sites.
- Never add anyone you aren't sure was in this class, and nothing about
  anyone's children or family.
- Don't contact anyone. The organizer reaches classmates through the class
  page and the app's messages.
